Between memory and movement

We don't design for the moment. We design for what comes after it.

Every piece is built with intention — from the silhouette to the smallest detail. Nothing is added just to fill space. Nothing is made to follow what's already been done.

Limited by design. Recognisable without a logo. Made to be worn, remembered, and eventually become part of something bigger.

No seasons. No noise. Just pieces worth keeping.
